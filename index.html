About Saburi Ply

Saburi Ply is a trusted plywood manufacturer in India known for superior quality, innovation, and sustainable craftsmanship.

Founded in 1990 by Mr Gajanand Munka, Saburi Ply is a combination of modern technology and traditional values which delivers a versatile range of products, including premium plywood, blockboards, pre-laminated (Mowud) boards, WPC and PVC panels, flush doors, and NRFC eco-panels (Neowud). As a trusted plywood supplier in India, we cater to the changing demands of architects, homeowners, interior designers, and industry professionals.

Our products are crafted for durability, strength, and environmental responsibility, which help create healthier homes and workspaces while upholding our legacy of trust and excellence.
